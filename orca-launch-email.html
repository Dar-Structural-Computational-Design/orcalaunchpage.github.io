ORCA is here.

Structural Automation and Design Assistant.

We are pleased to announce the launch of ORCA, a structural automation and design assistant developed by the Computational Design Team to support structural engineers in ETABS-based workflows.

ORCA brings multiple engineering capabilities into one organized platform, including automation, visualization, model adjustment, design checks, slab and raft workflows, seismic/lateral review, and BOQ-related tools.
